  Work in Green Chemistry
date: 2024-09-11T11:00:00.000-05:00

  sustainability.

Nasrin Hooshmand has been recognized by the [American Chemical Society](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/american-chemical-society_acssustainabilitystars-greenchemistry-activity-7237097111459090432-x2ru?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios) as a **“Sustainability Star”** for her work in promoting sustainability in chemistry. As an Assistant Professor at Texas A&M-Corpus Christi, this honor reinforces her commitment to advancing green chemistry and environmental sustainability, particularly through her research on innovative nanocomposite solutions for cleaner oceans and a healthier planet.
