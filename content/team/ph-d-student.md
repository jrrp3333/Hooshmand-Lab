Sai Priya Ramini is a Ph.D. student in Coastal and Marine System Science at Texas A&M University Corpus Christi. Her research interests focus on applying artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision techniques to scientific and environmental research. Her current research explores AI-assisted analysis of nanomaterials and nanoparticle characterization using experimental data such as microscopy images and spectral information. She also serves as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, supporting undergraduate chemistry laboratory courses.
