Manidhar holds an M.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University Corpus Christi and has since turned his focus toward applying deep learning to real-world environmental problems. His work has equipped him with skills in dataset validation, computer vision pipeline design, and rigorous model evaluation. Joining Dr. Nasrin's lab gave him the opportunity to put machine learning to work on a problem with direct environmental impact identifying micro and nanoplastic polymer types straight from microscope imagery, with the goal of cutting down how often the lab needs to rely on costly Raman and SERS spectroscopy.
