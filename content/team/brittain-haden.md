Brittain Haden holds an undergraduate degree in Psychology from Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi and is currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Chemistry. During her undergraduate studies, she spent one and a half years conducting research in Dr. Nasrin Hooshmand’s lab, developing experience in nanoparticle synthesis and characterization, including the fabrication of gold nanoparticles. During this time, she explored gold nanoparticle morphology and its relationship to cancer photothermal therapy. Her current work continues to explore how nanomaterials can be designed and characterized for potential applications in cancer treatment and immunological research.
